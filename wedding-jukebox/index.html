Our Story

MMXIX

The Introduction

A crowded record shop on a rainy afternoon; two hands reached for the same pressing of "Diamond Life." He insisted she take it. She insisted he walk her home. Neither has walked anywhere alone since.

MMXXI

The Courtship

Long dinners that outlasted the candles, slow dances in a narrow kitchen, and a record player that never once went cold. A love unhurried, and all the deeper for it.

MMXXV

The Proposal

At the jazz bar where it all began, the band eased into "Is It a Crime." Before the first chorus had resolved, he was on one knee — and she had already answered.

MMXXVI

The Wedding Day

And now the finest verse of all. They would be honoured beyond measure to have you beside them as it begins.

Good things to know

Questions

When should I arrive?

Doors open at half past five in the evening; the ceremony begins promptly at six. Allow a few minutes for the valet and the coat check.

May I bring a guest?

The seats held in your name — revealed when you reply — already include any companions reserved for you. The number you see is yours to fill.

Are children invited?

This is an evening for the grown folks — dim lights, late hours, slow songs. We hope the little ones will forgive us this one record.

Will the evening be photographed?

Yes — an unplugged ceremony, if you please. Leave the phones sleeping during the vows; the photographers will share everything, and the gallery above will fill soon after.

May I request a song?

You may, and you should — there is a line for it in your reply. The jukebox takes requests seriously.

Whom do I contact with questions?

Write to the couple at hello@ameliaandjames.example and they will answer between dance rehearsals.
