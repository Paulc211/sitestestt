Frequently asked questions

Help Center

What should I wear?

Formal attire — suits, cocktail dresses, or formal gowns. The theme colors are rosa and gold, so feel free to coordinate. Please leave white and blush pink for the star of the show.

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation says "and guest," absolutely! Just include them in your seat count when you RSVP.

Are children welcome?

¡Claro que sí! This is a family celebration — kids are welcome at both the misa and the reception. Just count every seat, big or small, in your RSVP.

Do I need to attend the misa?

We would love to have you at both, but if you can only make the reception, the doors of the Grand Salon open at 5:00 PM. The vals starts at 6:30 sharp — you won't want to miss it.

What about gifts?

Your presence at the premiere is the best gift. If you'd like to give something more, there will be a money-dance and a gift table at the reception.

Will there be a surprise dance?

That would be a spoiler. (Yes. Episode 5. Be on the dance floor by 9:00 PM.)
