02 — HEAR IT? FEEL IT?

What your rear end is trying to tell you.

Pick the symptom that matches. We'll tell you what it usually means, how urgent it is, and what the fix looks like.

Don't ignore it

Ring & pinion wear or bad setup

A whine that changes pitch with throttle is the classic gear-set sound. Either the ring and pinion are worn, or backlash and pinion depth were never set right. It usually gets louder over months, then bearings start to go.

  • Louder on acceleration = drive side of the tooth
  • Louder on deceleration = coast side of the tooth
  • Common after a low-fluid event or a rushed re-gear
Typical fixPattern check and re-shim if caught early. Ring & pinion plus a full bearing kit if the teeth are scored.
Get it looked at

Excess backlash or a worn spider gear set

That thud when you shift from park to drive or get on and off the gas is slop somewhere in the driveline. In the diff it's usually worn spider gears, thrust washers, or backlash that's opened up.

  • We also check U-joints and yoke splines so you don't pay for the wrong part
  • Very common on trucks that see burnouts or towing
Typical fixCarrier rebuild or a fresh posi unit. Backlash reset to spec.
Stop driving on it

Pinion or carrier bearing failure

A steady roar that tracks with road speed is almost always a bearing. Once it starts, the gear set is riding on a moving target and the damage snowballs fast.

  • Changes when you swerve left or right = wheel or axle bearing
  • Doesn't change with steering = pinion or carrier bearing
Typical fixFull bearing kit and seals. If caught early the gears are usually saved.
Easy if you catch it

Pinion seal, axle seal or cover gasket

Gear oil is thick and smells like sulfur, you'll know it. A leaking pinion seal shows up at the nose of the diff, axle seals show up inside the wheel and can soak your brakes.

  • Low fluid is the number one killer of otherwise healthy diffs
  • We set pinion preload correctly when replacing the seal, not just tighten the nut
Typical fixSeal, fluid and friction modifier. Usually same-day.
Upgrade, not a repair

Open diff or a worn-out posi

An open differential sends power to the tire with the least grip. If your truck came with a limited-slip and now only one tire spins, the clutches are worn. Either way, there's a fix that fits how you drive.

  • Street and towing: clutch or helical limited-slip
  • Trail and desert: selectable or automatic locker
Typical fixPosi rebuild kit, or a new limited-slip or locker unit installed and set up.
Performance, not damage

Your gearing no longer matches your tires

Taller tires effectively make your gears taller too. The engine falls out of its power band, the trans hunts on hills and your mileage tanks. A re-gear puts the RPM back where the factory intended.

  • Rule of thumb: every 2 inches of tire adds about one ratio step
  • We can reprogram the speedometer and shift points on most trucks
Typical fixRing & pinion swap, front and rear on 4x4s. Use the calculator to see your numbers.
