Questions

What should I wear?

Cocktail noir — velvet, silk, jewel tones, black tie welcome. Dress like you're stepping into a Sade record, because you are.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation's seat count (revealed when you RSVP) already includes any guests we've reserved for you — the number you see is yours to fill.

Are children welcome?

This one's an evening for the grown folks — dim lights, late hours, and slow songs. Little ones sit this record out.

Where should we stay?

Room blocks are held at the Ace and the Hoxton, both a short walk down Broadway. Mention "A & J wedding" when booking.

What about gifts?

Your presence on the dance floor is the gift. If you insist, a registry link will appear right here soon.
