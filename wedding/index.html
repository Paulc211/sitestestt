Good things to know

Questions

What should I wear?

Garden formal — think flowing dresses, linen suits, soft colors. The ceremony is on grass, so consider block heels or flats.

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation says "and guest," absolutely! Just include them in your seat count when you RSVP.

Are children welcome?

We adore your little ones, but this will be an adults-only evening — with the exception of family in the wedding party.

Where should we stay?

We've reserved room blocks at the Vineyard Inn and Hotel Solaine, both ten minutes away. Mention "A & J wedding" when booking.

What about gifts?

Your presence is truly the present. If you'd like to give something, a registry link will live right here soon.
